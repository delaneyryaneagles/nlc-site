Bathroom Remodel: Tiled Shower With a Niche

Five photos, in order: the wall tile going up, the shower finished with its niche, then the room primed and painted.

Google Reviews

One bathroom, start to finish.

This bathroom got a new tiled shower with a niche, a tile floor, and fresh paint. The photos below follow the job in order, from the first rows of wall tile to the room masked off for paint.

We're licensed in all three states: PA HIC #PA201433, DE #2026700487 and NJ HIC #13VH14325000. Family-run, over 50 years in the trade, and fully insured.

Step by step.

01

Set the wall tile

The shower walls go up one row at a time, with leveling clips at the joints so the tile sits flat and the lines stay straight. The niche is framed into the back wall as the tile goes up.

02

Finish the shower and floor

Shower tile and floor tile go in. The niche gets its own accent tile and a ledge. The shower pan stays covered in plastic until the finish work is done.

03

Prep and paint

With the tile done, the shower gets masked off in plastic. Then the walls are primed and painted. The new tile stays covered the whole time.

Most bathrooms fail behind the tile.

Most failed bathrooms fail behind the tile: no real waterproofing, a flat shower pan, a fan vented into the attic. We build the parts you won't see to last, then finish what you will.

That's why the prep matters as much as the tile. The finished shower is only as good as what's under it.
