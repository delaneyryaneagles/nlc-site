Basement Finish: From Bare Framing to Ready for Paint

Photos in order: framing and insulation, drywall, lights, primer, and a room ready for paint.

Google Reviews

Bare framing to a room you can paint.

This page walks through a basement finish the way the job goes: framing and insulation first, then drywall, lights, primer, and walls ready for paint. The first photos show the earlier stages. The last four are from our current basement job.

We're licensed in all three states: PA HIC #PA201433, DE #2026700487 and NJ HIC #13VH14325000. Family-run, over 50 years in the trade, and fully insured.

The earlier stages.

01

Frame and insulate

Walls get framed and insulated before any board goes up. This is the part you never see again, so it gets done right.

02

Hang and tape

Drywall goes up, then the seams and screws get taped and mudded.

03

Lights in

Ceiling lights go in with the drywall, so the room is lit the way it'll be used.

04

Spray the primer

Everything gets covered, and primer goes on with a sprayer for an even coat.
